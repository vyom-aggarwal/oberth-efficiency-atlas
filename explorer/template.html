Oberth Flyby Explorer

A powered flyby, simulated live: how much of the impulsive Oberth bonus a finite burn keeps (η), what it costs in equivalent Δv, and where the case sits on the universal loss curve and the η atlas. Prograde or fixed-direction thrust; one constant-thrust burn centred on periapsis.

Planar two-body problem with point-mass gravity; one constant-thrust burn with the rocket mass loss; Dormand–Prince 5(4) integration at a 10−12 tolerance. This JavaScript simulator reproduces the project's Python simulator to 2×10−10 in η on ten reference cases. η is undefined for captured arrivals; burns that start outside the body's sphere of influence are flagged, because the planet-centred model does not apply there. Engine and body ranges are the project's representative presets, not hardware data.
